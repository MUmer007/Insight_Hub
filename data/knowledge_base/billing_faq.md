# Billing and Account FAQ

## Payment Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and Apple Pay. We do not accept checks, money orders, or cryptocurrency.

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails, your order is not placed and no charge occurs. Common causes include insufficient funds, an expired card, or your bank flagging the transaction as suspicious. Try a different payment method or contact your bank.

## Subscription Billing
For subscription products, billing occurs automatically every 30 days from your signup date. You'll receive an email receipt after each charge. You can view your next billing date under Account Settings > Subscriptions.

## Cancelling a Subscription
Cancel anytime from Account Settings > Subscriptions > Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep access until then, and you won't be charged again.

## Updating Payment Information
Go to Account Settings > Payment Methods to add, remove, or update a card. Changes apply to your next billing cycle automatically.

## Duplicate Charges
If you notice what looks like a duplicate charge, it's often a temporary "authorization hold" from your bank that will drop off within 3-5 business days and was never an actual charge. If a genuine duplicate charge persists past 5 business days, contact support with both transaction IDs for a refund.

## Promo Codes
Promo codes must be applied at checkout before payment — they cannot be applied retroactively to a completed order. Only one promo code can be used per order.
