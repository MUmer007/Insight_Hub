# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Times
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the continental US. Express shipping (additional fee) takes 2-3 business days. International shipping takes 10-21 business days depending on destination.

## Shipping Costs
Orders over $50 ship free with standard shipping. Orders under $50 have a flat $6.99 shipping fee. Express shipping costs an additional $14.99 regardless of order size.

## Order Tracking
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email within 24 hours. You can track your order status anytime by logging into your account and visiting "Order History."

## Delayed or Lost Packages
If your package hasn't arrived within the expected delivery window, contact support with your order number. We investigate delayed packages after 3 business days past the expected delivery date. Lost packages confirmed by the carrier are replaced free of charge or refunded, customer's choice.

## International Shipping
We currently ship to the US, Canada, UK, and EU countries. International orders may be subject to customs fees and import taxes, which are the customer's responsibility and are not included in our shipping charges.

## Address Changes
Address changes can only be made within 1 hour of placing an order, before the order enters processing. Contact support immediately if you need to change a shipping address.
